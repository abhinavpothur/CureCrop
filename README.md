# CureCrop 🌿

An end-to-end, AI-powered crop disease diagnosis and automated pesticide delivery platform designed to support farmers from detection to cure.

## 💡 How It Works
1. **Scan & Detect:** The farmer uploads or captures a photo of an affected crop leaf.
2. **AI Diagnosis:** The AI identifies the exact plant disease in real time.
3. **Prescription & Dosage:** The system calculates the specific pesticide needed and the exact quantity/dosage required.
4. **Doorstep Delivery:** The farmer orders the treatment directly through the platform, and the required supplies are delivered to their location.

## 🛠️ Built With
- React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- Node.js & Express
- Google Gemini AI
- Google Maps Platform
